# Real-World Use Cases & Decision Playbooks

SpotLift bridges the gap between raw broadcast logs (Schaltpläne) and business decisions. Rather than presenting abstract mathematical formulas in isolation, these playbooks walk through concrete campaign workflows tailored to specific practitioner personas.

---

| Persona | Core Business Problem | Relevant Playbook | Key Deliverable |

| **Regional / Franchise Brand** | *"How do we measure local spot TV without diluting the signal across national baseline traffic?"* | [Regional Spot TV & Geo-Holdouts](/use-cases/regional-spot-tv-holdouts) | State/DMA segmentation with unexposed control state verification |
